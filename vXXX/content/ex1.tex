\section{Reflectance Measurements}
\label{sec:Auswertung}

As reflection plays an important role in the losses of solar cells, we measure the absorption for different pv materials. This is 
done by using a reflection chamber, in which all the light, which is not absorbed by the pv material, is reflected and measured.
This data is shown in the first plot of \ref{fig:reflection}. The second plot shows the refractive index of the materials. As 
one can easily see, the refractive index of the material depends heavily on the wavelength.

\begin{figure}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{reflections.pdf}
  \caption{Reflected light of the different pv materials.}
  \label{fig:reflection}
\end{figure}

In the first plot there is an oscillation-like behaviour of the reflection of the amorphous material. This is caused by 
interference caused by the structure of the material. In thin films, light is reflected bothn on the surface and on the 
back of a probe. With the light incoming normally, the optical path difference is equal to $2 n_2 d$, where $n_2$ is the 
refractive index of the material and $d$ is the thickness of the material. With this, constructive interference occurs at multiples 
of the wavelength $\lambda$.
\begin{equation}
  2 n_2 d = (m-\dfrac{1}{2})\lambda
\end{equation}
